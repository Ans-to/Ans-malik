# Per-clause narration and scene locking

The six selected-voice WAVs in `assets/clause-voice` are generated separately, at natural speed. `clause_audio.concatenate` inserts exactly 180 ms of zero PCM samples between clips (and 180 ms before the first clip for pre-start QA), records integer sample boundaries, and makes no cuts or speed changes. The WAV boundaries include any leading/trailing silence supplied by the voice synthesizer, so they are exact *clip* boundaries, not exact first-phoneme onsets. The final browser-compatible MP4 encodes the PCM to AAC; its compressed audio is not bit-identical to the source WAV.

`clause_render.py` passes those boundaries to Chromium. Scene K is hidden before its start and starts unfolding on the first frame at or after that boundary (up to one 60-fps frame later). Since a transition takes time, a scene cannot be fully revealed on the exact instant its transition starts; QA checks its midpoint, not full visibility for every instant in the interval. The renderer checks scene visibility on all frames. `qa_inspector.py --clauses` verifies the exact PCM segments and gaps and extracts both pre-start and midpoint MP4 frames, assembling six labeled midpoint samples. Its DOM check establishes timing gates; it is not image recognition.

The user-requested verbatim script contains material factual errors: the 88% figure is USD involvement on one side of 2022 foreign-exchange trades, not all global trade; gold did not physically exit all vaults or get replaced by digital ledgers in 1971. “Bretton Woods Monetary Act” is not the name of the 1971 US action. These are presented here only because the requested script and card wording are exact; they should not be treated as historical claims. Sources: https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/gold-convertibility-ends and https://www.bis.org/press/p221027.htm.

## Continuous motion QA

Each frame is rendered independently in Chromium. Scene-specific transforms, the pen, vault, ledger rows, globe and counter are evaluated from clamped clause-relative progress; ambient lights, bokeh and the moving fiber-grid also continue during audio gaps. The 18-image contact sheet samples 20%, 50% and 80% through each clause. The encoded MP4 is fully decoded in RGB, and every neighboring pair is measured at full resolution; the build fails unless at least 98% exceed a mean absolute delta of 0.65, no two consecutive deltas are below 0.20, and no consecutive frames are exactly identical. This whole-frame metric detects holds but cannot prove every individual prop moves on every frame. The moving fiber-grid contributes to the aggregate delta and is visibly part of the design; the scene-specific motion should also be assessed in the strip.
